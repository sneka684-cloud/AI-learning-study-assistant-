COLLEGE REGULATIONS – STUDENT SUPPORT INFORMATION

General Regulations

1. Students should follow the rules and regulations of the college.

2. Students should maintain the required attendance as specified by the applicable academic regulations.

3. Students must follow the instructions given by the department and faculty members.

4. Students are expected to complete academic activities, assignments and project work within the announced deadlines.

Examination Regulations

5. Students must follow the examination rules and instructions issued by the college.

6. Students should carry the required examination materials and identity documents as instructed by the institution.

7. Students should reach the examination venue on time.

8. Students must maintain proper discipline during examinations.

Academic Support

9. Students should contact the appropriate faculty member or department for official academic clarification.

10. Students should verify important academic information using official college sources.

Important Note

This file contains sample information prepared for the internship project demonstration. It should not be treated as an official college regulation. Students should always verify current rules with their institution.
